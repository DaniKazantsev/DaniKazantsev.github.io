About

I am a PhD student in Machine Learning at MBZUAI (expected 2028), in the Statistical Machine Learning Group advised by Dr. Maxim Panov. My research is on Uncertainty Quantification and Conformal Prediction.

Before the PhD, I spent 5+ years in industry: as a quantitative researcher in high-frequency trading (FPGA-based strategies, crypto market making, DeFi) and as an anti-fraud data scientist at a ride-hailing company.

I hold a BSc and MSc in Applied Mathematics and Informatics from HSE University and graduated from the Yandex School of Data Analysis (YSDA). Before that, I studied at School #57 in Moscow. I am a former mathematical olympiad competitor and now serve on the committee of the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI).

Publications

  1. UAI2026

    Adaptive Cumulative Mass Calibration with Conformal Prediction

    D. Kazantsev, E. Moulines, M. Panov, N. Kotelevskii, M. Guizani

    Introduces cumulative mass calibration with the CMCE and α-CMCE error measures, and an adaptive temperature scaling tuned per input via conformal prediction with guaranteed marginal coverage. Improves ECE, cw-ECE and MCE over baselines, especially on image classification with many classes.

    PMLR
  2. Preprint2026

    Frozen FjORD: Faster Federated Learning via Staged Frozen Ordered Dropout

    D. Kazantsev, M. Guizani, L. Lukas, S. Horváth,
    M. Panov, E. Gorbunov, N. Kotelevskii

    A staged freezing mechanism for federated learning with heterogeneous clients that reduces communication costs and converges faster than Ordered Dropout.
